# SCRIPT — quantum-computing-short

**Voice:** am_michael (Kokoro, local)
**Voice settings:** default
**Voice direction:** Confident, fast, punchy — a science-Short narrator. Lands each phrase as its own beat; no filler breaths.

---

## Line 1 — Hook (Frame 1)

**Time:** 0.0 – 5.0s
**Delivery:** Cold open, statement then the turn. Hit "both" hard.

    Your computer thinks in zeros and ones. Quantum computers think in both — at the same time.

## Line 2 — The spinning coin (Frame 2)

**Time:** 5.0 – 13.0s
**Delivery:** Teaching tone, clear phrase boundaries: flat coin — spinning coin — the term.

    A normal bit is a coin lying flat — heads or tails. A qubit is that coin spinning — both at once. That's superposition.

## Line 3 — Exponential explosion (Frame 3)

**Time:** 13.0 – 21.0s
**Delivery:** Escalating, each number bigger than the last; awe on the final claim.

    Every qubit you add doubles the power. Ten qubits — a thousand states. Three hundred? More states than there are atoms in the entire universe.

## Line 4 — What it cracks (Frame 4)

**Time:** 21.0 – 29.0s
**Delivery:** Confident momentum; the three items as clean separate cues.

    That could crack problems supercomputers would need millions of years for. Designing new medicines. New materials. Unbreakable codes.

## Line 5 — The catch (Frame 5)

**Time:** 29.0 – 38.0s
**Delivery:** Drop the energy for "the catch?", then rebuild tension; relish "colder than deep space."

    The catch? Qubits are so fragile, one tiny vibration destroys the magic. So quantum computers live in freezers colder than deep space.

## Line 6 — Payoff (Frame 6)

**Time:** 38.0 – 44.0s
**Delivery:** Slow, certain, final. A beat between the two sentences.

    The next computing revolution isn't coming. It's already being built.
